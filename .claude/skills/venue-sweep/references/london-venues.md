## Per-venue notes

**Haggerston School** — the default recommendation for East/Shoreditch firms.
Openings almost every Tue/Thu/Fri at 18:00-20:00, often Mon/Wed too. The only
one of the three that can take a firm of 20+, because the 5-a-side option splits
into 3 courts. Courts on the same hour book independently, so check how many are
free, not whether any is.

**Old Street Skyway** — single court, 5-a-side only, so it caps at ~12 players.
Reliable midweek, mostly Tue and Thu. Renders **no cells at all** on Fri/Sat/Sun:
the venue does not sell those days. Flat £79.

**Mint Street** — calendar rendered empty for all nine weeks swept: no free
cells and no BOOKED cells either. That signals no online availability
configured, not a sold-out venue. Unconfirmed as of 2026-09-18. Call
**020 3589 4612** before ruling it out or quoting it to a firm.

## Gaps

* No weekend coverage at any of the three. A firm wanting Saturday needs a venue
  not on this list.
* Nothing outside 18:00-20:00 — no lunchtime or post-9pm games.
* East London only. South/West/North firms need a fresh hireapitch search.
